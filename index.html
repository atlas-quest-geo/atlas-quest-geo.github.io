    A note on the map

    The Carta Mundi is a stylised, simplified, hand-written SVG in an equirectangular projection. Coastlines are deliberately approximate — recognisable, not survey-accurate — and country pins are rough positions, not official centroids. Borders are not drawn. Please don't navigate a ship with it.

    A note on the facts

    The gazetteer covers 195 countries (193 UN members plus the two observer states). Capitals, continents and currencies were checked carefully; where a fact is contested, in transition or genuinely ambiguous, it is left out rather than guessed, and that country simply sits out that quiz. Transcontinental countries skip the continent quiz. Flags are drawn only where they can be drawn faithfully with simple shapes (shown at a uniform 3:2 shape); flags with detailed emblems are skipped rather than drawn wrong.

    How saving works

    Each traveller gets a passport in localStorage: stamps, best scores per expedition and a date-keyed daily streak. Export a passport page as a PNG, or back up every traveller as JSON.
